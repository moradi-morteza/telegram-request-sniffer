# Code of Conduct (Simple & Natural Version)

## Our Commitment

We want this community to be a safe, friendly, and respectful place for everyone.
No matter who you are or where you come from, you should feel welcome here.

## Expected Behavior

Please try to:

* Be kind and respectful to others
* Listen to different opinions
* Give helpful feedback without attacking anyone
* Admit mistakes and learn from them
* Think about what benefits the whole community, not just yourself

Please avoid:

* Using sexual or offensive language
* Insulting, trolling, or attacking people
* Harassing others in public or private
* Sharing someone’s private information without permission
* Any behavior that would be considered unprofessional

## Responsibilities

Community leaders may edit or remove content or take action when someone breaks these rules.
They’ll try to be fair and explain their decisions when needed.

## Where This Applies

These rules apply everywhere within the community, including online spaces and any situation where someone represents the community.

## Reporting Problems

If you see or experience inappropriate behavior, you can report it using the email address in the `package.json` file.
We’ll review the report responsibly and keep your privacy respected.

## Consequences

Depending on the situation, consequences may include:

1. **Reminder** – A friendly note to stop the behavior
2. **Warning** – A stronger message with temporary restrictions
3. **Temporary ban** – No participation for a specific time
4. **Permanent ban** – Removal from the community for repeated or severe issues
